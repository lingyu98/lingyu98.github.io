About Me

Music

I am a rapper and hip-hop producer in my spare time. I have been a NetEase artist under the stage name Mikeyboi 迈克男孩 since 2017. I write lyrical rap and make beats (see below for selected tracks featuring collaborators). Representing Nanjing University Hip-Hop Association from 2017-2021 left me with some of the best memories of my college years.

I also produced the background music for the intro video to our Human-AI Teaming platform CREW.
